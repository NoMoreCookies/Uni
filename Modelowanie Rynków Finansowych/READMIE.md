# Financial Market Modelling

This repository contains materials, notes, and coursework developed for the **Financial Market Modelling** course.  
The project focuses on the mathematical and financial foundations used to analyze fixed-income instruments, evaluate investment projects, and understand the time value of money.

## Course Overview

The course introduces core concepts of financial mathematics and market modelling, with particular emphasis on **bonds**, **cash flow analysis**, and **investment valuation techniques**.  
It combines theoretical background with practical calculations commonly used in finance.

The main topics covered in this repository include:

- **Bonds and fixed-income securities**
  - coupon bonds
  - zero-coupon bonds
  - bond pricing principles

- **Cash flows and the time value of money**
  - capitalization
  - discounting
  - present and future value of cash flows

- **Investment appraisal methods**
  - Net Present Value (**NPV**)
  - Internal Rate of Return (**IRR**)

- **Interest rate structure**
  - spot rates
  - forward rates

- **Bond risk measurement**
  - Macaulay duration

## Learning Goals

The purpose of this repository is to document and organize the key financial concepts discussed during the course, as well as to provide worked examples and practical exercises related to:

- valuing fixed-income instruments,
- comparing investment projects,
- interpreting discounting and compounding mechanisms,
- analyzing the term structure of interest rates,
- measuring the sensitivity of bond prices to time and yield changes.

## Repository Content

The repository may include:

- lecture notes,
- problem sets,
- R Markdown files,
- calculations and numerical examples,
- course-related visualizations and plots.

## Summary

Overall, this course provides a solid introduction to the quantitative tools used in financial market analysis, especially in the area of **bond valuation**, **cash flow modelling**, and **investment decision-making**.
